The work views show what the agents did on the host.

<Screenshot id="phone-session-menu" alt="A task session with the Session actions menu open: Task Details, Copy Task ID, Changes, Files, Artifacts, CI Checks, and Terminals.">

Open them from the **Session actions** menu of a session, or from the **Work** card of the [task details](/mobile/tasks/#task-details):

- [Changes](#changes): the changed files and their diffs.
- [Files](#files): the files of the worktree.
- [Artifacts](#artifacts): the documents of the task.
- [CI Checks](#ci-checks): the pull request and its checks.
- [Terminals](#terminals): the shells of the worktree.

**Artifacts** and **CI Checks** show only for a task.

</Screenshot>

The phone does not stage, commit, or push. The agents commit their work, and the push comes with the [approval](/mobile/tasks/#approve).

When a rebase or a merge stops on a conflict, **Changes** shows the conflicted files. Tap <Action>Abort rebase</Action> or <Action>Abort merge</Action> to go back. Or tap <Action>Resolve conflicts</Action>, then <Action>Send request</Action>, to ask the Builder to fix it.

## Comment on a diff [#comment]

<ProductDemo id="mobile-diff-comment" />

<Steps>
<Step>In **Changes**, tap a file to show its diff, or open its full diff.</Step>
<Step>Tap a line. To comment on more lines, tap the last line of the range.</Step>
<Step>Tap <Action>Comment</Action>, write what to change, and tap <Action>Comment</Action> again. The comment shows **Pending**.</Step>
<Step>Tap <Action>Send</Action>. The session opens, and your comments go with your next message.</Step>
</Steps>

## Files [#files]

<Screenshot id="phone-files" alt="The file viewer showing src/ui/Sidebar.tsx with line numbers.">

**Files** shows the folders of the worktree, with the Git status of each file. Each folder opens on its own screen. Search finds the files in the folder and below it.

The viewer shows:

- code with line numbers,
- Markdown as **Preview** or **Source**,
- images.

The phone has no editor.

</Screenshot>

## CI checks [#ci-checks]

**CI Checks** shows the pull request of the task, its checks, and its review comments, as on the desktop. To hide the resolved comments, open **Filter comments** and select **Hide resolved**. The same menu filters the comments by author. The view is read-only. See [CI checks](/git-and-pull-requests/#ci-checks).

## Artifacts [#artifacts]

**Artifacts** lists the documents of the workflow, such as the spec, the plan, and the QA report, and the free artifacts of the task. A free artifact shows a preview, or <Action>Share</Action> when the phone cannot show it. An HTML file shows as source: the app never runs it. To open a file in another app, tap <Action>Share</Action>. To add, rename, or delete a free artifact, use the desktop.

## Terminals [#terminals]

**Terminals** lists the open terminals of the task or the session. Tap <Action>New terminal</Action> to open a shell in the worktree, or start a [repository action](/customize-openducktor/#add-repository-actions). You set up the actions on the desktop.
